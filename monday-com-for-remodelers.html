Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Monday.com account?
You'll need one in your own name, so you own the system. We'll tell you which plan fits your team.
Will my crew actually use it?
That's the point of building it around how your team already works. We keep it simple, and include training.
Can it connect to GoHighLevel or my CRM?
Yes. When a deal is marked Won, the job can be created in Monday.com automatically with all its details.
Does this replace Buildertrend?
It can, for companies that find Buildertrend too heavy. If Buildertrend works well for you, we'd suggest keeping it and fixing the lead side instead.
