Where it leaks

Selling the job is only half the work.

After the contract signs, money leaks in three places.

1. Jobs stall after install.

Milestones wait on PTO, inspections or a rejected funding submission, and nobody finds out until weeks later.

2. Deals cancel in the quiet weeks.

Between signing and install, homeowners hear nothing. Doubt creeps in, and some cancel.

3. Two boards, two stories.

Sales tracks one thing and funding tracks another, so nobody can say how much money is actually on the way.

What we fix

One clear view of every job and every dollar.
