My story

From fixing one board to running whole systems.

I started freelancing in 2022 with GoHighLevel, then moved deep into monday.com. Over the next four years I built systems for about 200 clients on Fiverr: sales CRMs, crew schedules, manufacturing workflows, event systems and integrations with tools like Xero, Acuity, PandaDoc and Samsara.

The same problem kept showing up. Small teams lose money in the gaps: a lead that waits too long, a job detail lost between sales and the crew, a payment nobody chases.

So I started Thrivebuild to fix those gaps for solar and home energy companies. Since early 2025 I've managed the monday.com sales CRM of a US solar sales company, and that work shapes everything we build.

I trained as an industrial chemist at Obafemi Awolowo University, and I'm based in Lagos, Nigeria, working with teams in the US, Canada, the UK and Australia.
