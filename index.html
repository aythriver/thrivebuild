The Bottlenecks

Where residential remodeling operations break down.

When you run $1M–$10M in volume with 5–30 field staff, standard spreadsheets and email inboxes fail in three predictable places:

1. Leads go cold in hours.

Homeowners submitting kitchen or addition inquiries call 3 competitors. If your office takes 4 hours to call back while you're on a job site, you've already lost the bid to whoever answered in 5 minutes.

2. Scope vanishes between sales & PMs.

Sales promises custom tile allowances, specific demolition dates, or parking rules. When the job signs, that context stays trapped in text messages, and your PM walks onto the job blind.

3. The owner has zero single-screen visibility.

You are texting lead carpenters at 9:00 PM just to know if materials arrived or inspections passed. You have no single dashboard telling you pipeline value, live job margins, or project delays.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our implementations, tooling, and pricing.

Does this replace Buildertrend or JobTread?
No, it enhances them. Construction management platforms like Buildertrend or JobTread are great for daily logs and purchase orders, but their CRM intake and custom sales-to-PM triggers are rigid. We bridge the front-end intake and job kickoff using Monday.com and Make.com, then sync cleanly to your construction software.
How long does a typical implementation take?
Single packages (like the Lead Follow-Up System or Handoff System) are built, tested, and handed over in 7 to 10 business days. The Full Company Operating System typically takes 2 to 3 weeks including team training and live testing with active jobs.
Do I need to set up Monday.com and Make.com accounts beforehand?
No. On the 45-minute Workflow Map call, we evaluate your existing licenses. If you already have Monday.com, we build inside your existing account. If not, we advise you on the exact tier needed (usually Monday Pro and Make Core) so you don't overspend on enterprise licenses.
Are there ongoing retainer fees or hidden costs?
Zero consulting retainers. Our systems are flat-rate, one-time project fees ($1,500, $2,500, or $5,000). You own the boards, scenarios, and documentation completely. You only pay your standard software subscription fees directly to Monday and Make.
Do you work with commercial general contractors?
No. Commercial construction involves AIA billing, complex lien waivers, and multi-tier submittals that require specialized enterprise ERPs. We stay 100% focused on residential kitchen, bath, whole-home, and outdoor living remodeling firms where fast homeowner response and rapid trade handoff win jobs.
Can we build just the lead follow-up system first?
Absolutely. Many remodelers start with the $1,500 Lead Follow-Up package to capture immediate ROI on missed inquiries. Once your speed-to-lead is locked down and consultations are consistently booking, we can add the Sale-to-Job Handoff System anytime without redoing past work.
