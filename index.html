The Bottlenecks

Where residential remodeling operations break down.

When you're running several jobs at once with a team of 5 to 30, spreadsheets and email inboxes break down in three predictable places:

1. Leads go cold in hours.

Homeowners asking about a kitchen or an addition usually contact more than one contractor. If your office calls back four hours later from a job site, the homeowner may already have booked with someone else.

2. Scope vanishes between sales & PMs.

Sales promises custom tile allowances, specific demolition dates, or parking rules. When the job signs, that context stays trapped in text messages, and your PM walks onto the job blind.

3. The owner has zero single-screen visibility.

You are texting lead carpenters at 9:00 PM just to know if materials arrived or inspections passed. You have no single dashboard telling you pipeline value, live job margins, or project delays.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our implementations, tooling, and pricing.

Does this replace Buildertrend or JobTread?
No. If your jobs already run well there, keep it. We add the lead follow-up system in front of it, and connect the two where the software allows. We confirm exactly what's possible on your Workflow Map call before we quote.
We pay for Buildertrend but the team barely uses it. Can you help?
Yes, and it's common. On the call, we look at why the team isn't using it. Sometimes a simpler back office built on Monday.com, around how your team already works, is the better fit. Sometimes keeping it and fixing the lead side is. We'll tell you honestly which one makes sense.
Why use more than one platform?
Each does one job well. GoHighLevel is built for answering and following up with homeowners by text, email and phone. Monday.com is built for running projects. Smart automation moves the job details from one to the other the moment a deal is won. Your office staff and project managers each work in the part that fits their role.
What does the monthly fee cover?
Your GoHighLevel account, texting and phone setup including the carrier registration required to send business texts in the US, plus updates, fixes and small changes. Text message and call usage is billed at cost based on your volume.
Who owns the system?
Your Monday.com boards and automations live in your own accounts and stay yours. Your GoHighLevel account runs under our agency. If you ever cancel, we export your contacts and pipeline data to you.
How long does it take?
The Lead Follow-Up System goes live in 7 to 10 business days. The Sale-to-Job Handoff System takes 2 to 3 weeks. The Full Company System takes 4 to 6 weeks, including training and testing with live jobs.
Do I need accounts set up beforehand?
No. We set up GoHighLevel for you. For Monday.com, we build inside your existing accounts, or recommend the right plan if you do not have one yet.
Can we start with just lead follow-up?
Yes. Many remodelers start with the Lead Follow-Up System. When you are ready, we add the handoff system on top without rebuilding anything.
Can you quote something that isn't one of the packages?
Yes. Book a free 20-minute Workflow Map call, and we'll send one fixed quote within 48 hours. Custom builds start from $1,500.
Is the AI receptionist a real person?
No. It is an AI voice agent that answers when your team cannot, collects the project details, and either books the estimate or passes the call to you. You decide what it says and when it hands off.
Do you work with commercial contractors?
Our systems are built for residential remodeling and design-build firms. Commercial work involves different billing and submittal processes. If you are a commercial contractor, email us and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.
