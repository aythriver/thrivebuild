The Bottlenecks

Where home improvement companies lose money.

It almost always happens in the same three places.

1. Leads go cold in hours.

Homeowners asking about a roof, solar or a kitchen contact more than one company. Call back four hours later and they may have already booked someone else.

Why leads go cold, plus 5 free texts →

2. Details vanish between sales & the job.

Sales promises a panel layout, a shingle color or an install date. After the contract signs, those details stay in someone's texts, and the crew walks onto the job blind.

3. The job is done, but the money isn't in.

Jobs finish, then sit waiting on funding or final payment while nobody is alerted. Money you've already earned stays stuck.

Who do you work with?
Solar, roofing, remodeling and design-build companies, usually with a team of 5 to 30. They share the same gaps: slow lead follow-up, messy handoffs from sales to the job, and payments nobody is chasing.
Do I have to switch software?
No. If you already have a CRM or job software that works, we fix and connect what you have. If you are on texts and spreadsheets, we build on GoHighLevel and monday.com. We confirm what makes sense on your Workflow Map call.
How long does it take?
Most fixes are done in 5 to 7 business days. Full systems take 1 to 6 weeks depending on scope. You get the exact timeline in your fixed quote.
Who owns the system?
Your monday.com boards and automations live in your own accounts and stay yours. GoHighLevel accounts run under our agency, and if you ever cancel, we export your contacts and pipeline data to you.
