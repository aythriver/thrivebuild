The Setup

What we set up

Instant replies

Every website form, ad lead or Google inquiry gets a friendly text and email within a minute, from your business name.

Missed-call text-back

A call you miss on a job site triggers a text right away, so the homeowner doesn't call the next remodeler on the list.

Online estimate booking

Homeowners pick their own estimate time from your calendar, with automatic reminders the day before.

Follow-up that stops when they reply

Short, human messages on day 1, 3 and 7 for anyone who hasn't booked.

A remodeling sales pipeline

New Inquiry, Contacted, Estimate Booked, Estimate Done, Proposal Sent, Won, Lost, so you always know where every lead stands.

Review requests

Sent automatically when a job is marked complete.

Who this is for

Residential remodelers, kitchen and bath remodelers and design-build firms that get leads from their website, Google, Houzz, Angi or referrals, and want every one of them answered fast, even during busy weeks.

Already using Buildertrend or JobTread?

Keep it. Construction software is great at running jobs. Fast lead follow-up is usually where it's thinner. GoHighLevel sits in front of it, and we connect the two where the software allows. We confirm what's possible on your Workflow Map call before quoting.
