At a glance

The short version

A US residential solar sales company runs its sales pipeline, its admin work and its upsells in monday.com. Since early 2025 we have built and managed that CRM: the boards, the integrations that move jobs between boards, and the native monday.com workflows that keep each stage in order.

Funding is tracked in a separate tool, so we set up alerts and views that show sales where each deal stands.

About the client

A US residential solar sales company

A residential solar sales company in the United States. Its sales team signs homeowners, and an admin side tracks funding and job progress after the sale. Upsells are tracked on their own board.

We keep the company's name private.

The challenge

Where things got stuck

Sales, upsells and jobs lived on separate boards, and the owner felt they weren't getting enough from the CRM for what it cost.

Funding is tracked in a separate tool, so sales had no simple way to see where each deal stood after it was signed.

Approach

How we did it

  1. 01

    Map the workflow

    We mapped how a deal moves from first contact to signed, then through funding and job progress, and where upsells fit in.

  2. 02

    Build and go live

    We set up the sales pipeline, the admin board and the upsells board, connected them with integrations and native monday.com workflows, and added alerts and views for sales.

  3. 03

    Ongoing support

    Since early 2025 we have managed the CRM month to month, adjusting boards, integrations and workflows as the business changes.

What we built

  • Sales pipeline CRM

    Every lead and deal in one monday.com pipeline.

  • Admin board

    Funding and job progress tracked after the sale.

  • Upsells board

    Matched to the main admin board.

  • Integrations

    Jobs move between boards without retyping.

  • Native workflows

    New jobs land at the top of each stage.

  • Alerts and views

    Sales can see where each deal stands.
