At a glance

The short version

NE Tiny Homes is a modular homes and design-build manufacturer. Its contracts, permit milestones, build phases and SOPs were spread across tools, spreadsheets and message threads.

We built a monday.com architecture that connects them in one system, including milestone payments from contract deposit to framing sign-off.

About the client

NE Tiny Homes

NE Tiny Homes designs and builds modular homes. Each home moves from a signed contract through permits and a series of build phases, with SOPs for the work at each step.

The challenge

Where things got stuck

Contracts, permits, build phases and SOPs lived in different tools, loose spreadsheets and message threads.

That made it hard to see in one place where each home stood and which milestone came next.

Approach

How we did it

  1. 01

    Map the workflow

    We mapped how a home moves from signed contract through permits and build phases, and where each SOP and milestone payment fits.

  2. 02

    Build and go live

    We built a monday.com architecture that connects contracts, permit milestones, build phases and SOPs, with milestone payments tracked from contract deposit to framing sign-off.

  3. 03

    Hand over

    The team works from one connected monday.com workspace for contracts, permits, build phases and SOPs.
