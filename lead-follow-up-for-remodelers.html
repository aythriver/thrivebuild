The Bottlenecks

Why remodeling leads go cold

Calls get missed on job sites

And most homeowners don't leave a voicemail.

Forms arrive after hours and on weekends

When nobody is in the office.

Follow-up depends on memory

So leads who don't book right away are forgotten.

No-shows aren't rebooked

And a good lead disappears quietly.

Free Templates

5 free texts you can use today

Swap in your details and use them as they are.

  1. 1. Missed call

    "Hi, it's [Your name] from [Company]. Sorry we missed your call, we're probably on a job site. What project are you thinking about? I'll get right back to you."

  2. 2. After-hours website form

    "Hi [First name], thanks for reaching out to [Company] about your [kitchen/bath] project! We're out of the office right now, but you can grab a time for your free estimate here: [booking link]. Talk soon, [Your name]"

  3. 3. No booking after 24 hours

    "Hi [First name], just checking in on your [project]. Most homeowners have a few questions before the estimate. Want me to give you a quick call, or would you rather pick a time here: [link]?"

  4. 4. Day-before reminder

    "Hi [First name], looking forward to seeing you tomorrow at [time] for your [project] estimate. If anything changes, just reply here. [Your name], [Company]"

  5. 5. No-show

    "Hi [First name], sorry we missed each other today. No problem at all, life gets busy. Here's a link to pick a new time that works better: [link]"
